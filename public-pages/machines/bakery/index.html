FAQ

Your questions, answered.

How do I choose suitable equipment?

Start with your product, process, intended output and available space. LS can review those requirements with you before confirming machine suitability.

Can LS support installation and training?

LS provides installation, technology transfer, maintenance and after-sales support. Discuss the services required for your equipment and production setup with the team.

What should I include in an inquiry?

Include your product, current process, requirements and a reply contact. When you start from a machine page, its model is carried into your email or WhatsApp message automatically.
