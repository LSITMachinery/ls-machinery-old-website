Website enquiry privacy notice

Website version: 15 October 2026.

01

Who handles your enquiry

LS Smart Machinery (M) SDN BHD (1308318-K) is the company you are contacting through this website. This notice explains the website enquiry routes and the information you choose to provide.

02

Information and purpose

Your enquiry may include your name, company, email or telephone, country, message, production plans and the machine or page you were viewing. A parts request may also include machine and part identifiers, quantity, delivery address and remarks. You provide this information directly so LS can discuss your request, prepare a quotation, arrange a visit or respond about service or parts. Do not include payment details, identity documents or sensitive personal information in these forms.

03

Your choices

The form requires a name, a message or part description, and at least one reply contact. Other fields are optional. Without a reply contact LS cannot contact you about the request. You can choose email, WhatsApp or an available website submission route. An enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

04

Email and WhatsApp

Opening an email draft or WhatsApp passes the enquiry text into the application or service you choose. Review and send it there. Opening the application does not send the request or confirm receipt by LS. Your email or messaging provider handles the information under its own terms and privacy notice.

05

Website submission

A website submission is available only when the delivery service is connected. Submitting sends the completed enquiry through the website server to the configured sales or marketing delivery service. An acceptance message means the delivery service accepted the request; it does not confirm that a person has read it. If submission is unavailable or fails, use the displayed email or WhatsApp option.

06

People and services involved

Your request is intended for LS sales, marketing or service staff handling the enquiry. Depending on the route you choose, website hosting, delivery, email or messaging providers also process the request. External applications have their own privacy terms and may process information outside Malaysia. Website fonts may load from Google Fonts; your browser contacts that provider when requesting the fonts. Avoid sharing information about another person unless you are authorised to do so.

07

Information kept in your browser

The form keeps entered information in the current browser session to help you recover it after opening another application. It is not an LS inbox. Clearing the form removes that session draft. Site language and motion choices, and content-studio drafts, may also be stored in this browser. You can remove browser storage through your browser settings. Removing a browser draft does not remove a request already sent by email, WhatsApp or website submission.

08

Access, correction and other requests

Contact LS using the details below to ask about an enquiry record, request access or correction, ask about storage or removal, or limit further contact. Include enough information to identify your request. LS may need to check your identity before acting on a personal-data request.

Notis privasi pertanyaan laman web

Versi laman: 15 Oktober 2026.

01

Pihak yang mengendalikan pertanyaan anda

LS Smart Machinery (M) SDN BHD (1308318-K) ialah syarikat yang anda hubungi melalui laman ini. Notis ini menerangkan saluran pertanyaan laman dan maklumat yang anda pilih untuk berikan.

02

Maklumat dan tujuan

Pertanyaan anda boleh merangkumi nama, syarikat, e-mel atau telefon, negara, mesej, rancangan pengeluaran serta mesin atau halaman yang dilihat. Permintaan alat ganti juga boleh merangkumi pengenalan mesin dan alat ganti, kuantiti, alamat penghantaran serta catatan. Anda memberikan maklumat ini secara terus supaya LS boleh membincangkan permintaan, menyediakan sebut harga, mengatur lawatan atau membalas tentang perkhidmatan atau alat ganti. Jangan masukkan butiran pembayaran, dokumen pengenalan atau maklumat peribadi sensitif dalam borang ini.

03

Pilihan anda

Borang memerlukan nama, mesej atau penerangan alat ganti, serta sekurang-kurangnya satu saluran untuk membalas. Medan lain adalah pilihan. Tanpa maklumat untuk membalas, LS tidak dapat menghubungi anda tentang permintaan tersebut. Anda boleh memilih e-mel, WhatsApp atau penghantaran laman yang tersedia. Pertanyaan tidak melanggan anda kepada surat berita.

04

E-mel dan WhatsApp

Membuka draf e-mel atau WhatsApp memindahkan teks pertanyaan ke aplikasi atau perkhidmatan pilihan anda. Semak dan hantar di sana. Membuka aplikasi tidak menghantar permintaan atau mengesahkan penerimaan oleh LS. Penyedia e-mel atau pemesejan mengendalikan maklumat mengikut terma dan notis privasinya sendiri.

05

Penghantaran melalui laman

Penghantaran melalui laman hanya tersedia apabila perkhidmatan penghantaran disambungkan. Penghantaran menghantar pertanyaan lengkap melalui pelayan laman kepada perkhidmatan penghantaran jualan atau pemasaran yang dikonfigurasi. Mesej penerimaan bermaksud perkhidmatan penghantaran menerima permintaan; ia tidak mengesahkan seseorang telah membacanya. Jika penghantaran tidak tersedia atau gagal, gunakan pilihan e-mel atau WhatsApp yang dipaparkan.

06

Pihak dan perkhidmatan yang terlibat

Permintaan anda ditujukan kepada kakitangan jualan, pemasaran atau perkhidmatan LS yang mengendalikan pertanyaan. Mengikut saluran yang dipilih, penyedia pengehosan laman, penghantaran, e-mel atau pemesejan turut memproses permintaan tersebut. Aplikasi luar mempunyai terma privasi sendiri dan mungkin memproses maklumat di luar Malaysia. Fon laman mungkin dimuatkan daripada Google Fonts; pelayar anda menghubungi penyedia itu apabila meminta fon. Elakkan berkongsi maklumat orang lain tanpa kebenaran.

Borang menyimpan maklumat yang dimasukkan dalam sesi pelayar semasa supaya anda boleh memulihkannya selepas membuka aplikasi lain. Ia bukan peti masuk LS. Anda boleh membuang storan pelayar melalui tetapan pelayar. Membuang draf pelayar tidak membuang permintaan yang telah dihantar melalui e-mel, WhatsApp atau penghantaran laman.
