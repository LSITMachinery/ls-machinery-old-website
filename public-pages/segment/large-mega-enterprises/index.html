Connected thinking for complex production.

A production line is a sequence of connected processes. Product requirements, existing equipment, movement through the factory and operator support all need to work together.

  • Line layout and integration
  • Process connections
  • Installation and technology transfer
ALIGN ENGINEERING AND PROCUREMENT

Define the connections before choosing the line.

Bring production, engineering and procurement into one brief, with a clear record of what the proposed line must connect to.

  1. 01Product and process boundaries

    Define product formats, the sequence of operations and the existing equipment that will remain in use.
