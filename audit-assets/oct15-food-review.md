Displayed WebP derivatives retain the source license and may be cropped by the page layout. Wikimedia's recommended 960/1280-pixel delivery was used after its original-file endpoint returned a rate-limit message; no blocked endpoint was repeatedly polled.

## MCD: real photographed examples

Published LS post 3083 (`mini-cookies-depositor-compact-baking-machine-machine-profile`) pairs `IMG_2623.jpg` with “Jenny Cookie” and `IMG_20250818_130309.jpg` with “Spiral Cookies.” The first is labelled generically as **Piped cookies**, avoiding an implication that Jenny Bakery endorses LS. Both photos visibly show tray-arranged piped cookies. Original supplied files are under `uploads/2025/11/`. Exact source excerpts are retained in `oct15-food-sql-excerpts.txt`.

`IMG_20250212_130544.jpg` shows round cookies on trays. Its source caption says chocolate-chip cookies, but visible inclusions cannot be established. It is therefore a general discovery image, without a machine assignment or a chocolate-chip recipe claim.

The source product grid also contains drop cookies, pineapple tarts, meringues, long cookies, macarons and eclairs. Some images are borrowed recipe-site images; other machine pages repeat sections. Those entries are not treated as a complete verified MCD capability list. An exhaustive list still needs LS nozzle, dough and trial records.
