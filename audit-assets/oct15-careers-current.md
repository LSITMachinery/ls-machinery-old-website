Internal evidence. Read-only research; no applications, messages, sign-ins or subscriptions were sent.

## Result and limits

`career-opportunities.js` supplies 14 deduplicated role and employment-type records retrieved from LS's Indeed employer listings. This is a broader, evidence-based selection than the previous two roles. It is **not a live vacancy feed, a headcount, or a guarantee that every role remains open**. The external platform and LS HR confirm availability and employment terms. `checkedAt` means the source was checked on this date, not that the vacancy was posted on this date.

Indeed profile variants disagree on totals and relative dates; retrieved snapshots include 19, 25, 26, 36 and 45 advertisements, with repeated titles. The bare employer-profile open was cached from last month, another search result from yesterday. Do not publish these totals, relative ages, “new”, “hiring today”, salaries, or the differing job-ad working hours. The user's confirmed public visit hours remain separate.

The 14 records cover engineering, commercial work, business support and internships. These four group labels are editorial navigation, not a claim about LS's formal department structure. Translated role names are website translations; original names remain in `name`. Short descriptions are paraphrased only from retrieved listing bodies. Roles whose body could not be retrieved have no invented description.

## Presentation recommendation

Use an editorial opportunities section divided into Engineering, Commercial, Business support and Internships, each with clean ruled rows rather than shopping cards. Each row has the role, employment type, “View role on Indeed” and “Email HR about this role”. Group controls should reveal all records without a fixed ten-role cap. Preserve names and direct role links in the local CMS; include an “All LS opportunities on Indeed” fallback. A single short public line can say “Explore the roles listed on our recruitment channel, or speak to LS HR.” Do not say the page automatically synchronizes with job platforms.
