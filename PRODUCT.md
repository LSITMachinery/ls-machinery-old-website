## Capabilities and constraints

The existing website uses vanilla JavaScript, generated public HTML and a Node preview/delivery adapter. Preserve the current routes, SEO metadata, catalogue search/filtering, image viewer, browser-local CMS demonstration, enquiry validation and contextual email/WhatsApp handoffs. Direct web submission remains conditional on an approved delivery provider. The CMS is not a shared authenticated publishing system.

## Brand commitments

Preserve the LS logo, orange/charcoal identity, genuine machinery and site photography, and credible industrial language. No fabricated specifications, customer results, testimonials or service coverage. Keep whole-machine images proportional and contained. No production deployment is authorised.
